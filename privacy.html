Privacy Policy

RevMax Publisher — a personal tool, used by one person, on their own Instagram account.

What this app is

RevMax Publisher schedules and publishes its owner's own Instagram Reels. It is not a product, it is not sold, and it has no other users.

What it does

  • Publishes Reels to the owner's own account, on dates the owner chooses
  • Posts comments the owner has written on the owner's own Reels
  • Reads the reach and engagement numbers of the owner's own Reels
  • Reads the comments on the owner's own Reels, to see who asked for something the owner offered
  • May send one private reply to a person who commented on the owner's Reel asking for that offer — only in answer to their comment

What it does not do

  • Read anything on any account other than the owner's.
  • Read the owner's direct messages.
  • Like, follow, or comment on anyone else's posts.
  • Sell, share, or use any data for advertising, profiling or training.

What it stores, and where

Each Reel's caption, its numbers, and the usernames of people who asked for the owner's offer in a comment are kept in the owner's own private Notion workspace and the owner's own private repository. Nothing is uploaded to any other third party. There is no analytics and no tracking.

Keeping and deleting data

The owner can delete everything at any time by deleting their Notion database and repository, and by removing the app's access from their Instagram settings (Settings and privacy → Website permissions → Apps and websites). Anyone who commented and wants their username removed can email the address below and it will be deleted.
